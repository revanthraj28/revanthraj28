## About me

I'm an AI engineer and technical lead in Bengaluru with 3+ years of building production agentic systems and applied-ML stacks end to end, along with the distributed backends that carry them.

Right now I lead AI architecture as a Senior AI Engineer at **Epiance**, across agentic platforms, physics-based simulation, GPS-denied navigation and on-prem model deployment. Before that I built GIS backends in C#/.NET, led a 7-engineer team on a government field platform, and started out in native iOS.
